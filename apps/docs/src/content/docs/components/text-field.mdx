## Label, help text and error text

`label` names the field and `help-text` is guidance below it. `error-text` replaces the browser's
validation message once the field is invalid after user interaction; it does not make the field
invalid by itself. Try leaving the second field empty after typing in it and clearing it.

<Demo
  code={`<neu-text-field label="Name" placeholder="Ada Lovelace" help-text="As on your passport"></neu-text-field>
<neu-text-field label="Nickname" required error-text="A nickname is required"></neu-text-field>`}
/>

## Prefix and suffix

The `prefix` and `suffix` slots hold content on either side of the input.

<Demo
  code={`<neu-text-field label="Price" placeholder="0.00">
  <span slot="prefix">$</span>
  <span slot="suffix">USD</span>
</neu-text-field>`}
/>

## In a form

A submit shows the form data and Reset restores the defaults.

<Demo
  code={`<form name="demo-form">
  <neu-text-field name="username" label="Username" required></neu-text-field>
  <neu-text-field name="email" type="email" label="Email" help-text="Enter your email"></neu-text-field>
  <neu-button type="submit" variant="primary">Submit</neu-button>
  <neu-button type="reset">Reset</neu-button>
  <output></output>
</form>`}
/>

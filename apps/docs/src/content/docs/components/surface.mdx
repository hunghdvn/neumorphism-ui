## Shapes

The surface can be raised, inset, flat, convex or concave to create different lighting effects.

<Demo
  code={`<neu-surface shape="raised">
  Raised
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface shape="inset">
  Inset
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface shape="flat">
  Flat
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface shape="convex">
  Convex
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface shape="concave">
  Concave
</neu-surface>`}
/>

## Elevation levels

Controls how far the surface appears to lift off its background.

<Demo
  code={`<neu-surface elevation="sm">
  Small elevation
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface elevation="md">
  Medium elevation
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface elevation="lg">
  Large elevation
</neu-surface>`}
/>

## Corner radius

Adjusts how rounded the corners are.

<Demo
  code={`<neu-surface radius="sm">
  Small radius
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface radius="md">
  Medium radius
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface radius="lg">
  Large radius
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface radius="full">
  Full radius
</neu-surface>`}
/>

## Padding

Sets the space between the edge and the content.

<Demo
  code={`<neu-surface padding="none">
  No padding
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface padding="sm">
  Small padding
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface padding="md">
  Medium padding
</neu-surface>
<neu-surface padding="lg">
  Large padding
</neu-surface>`}
/>

## Content

Wrap any content inside a surface component.

<Demo
  code={`<neu-surface shape="raised" elevation="md" padding="md">
  <h3>Card Title</h3>
  <p>This is a surface component containing arbitrary content.</p>
  <neu-button variant="primary">Action</neu-button>
</neu-surface>`}
/>
